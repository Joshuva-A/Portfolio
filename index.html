Plain-language control statements

PR-91 v2.0 distils eight governance domains into controls written so every team, not just security, can understand and apply them.

AI Impact Assessment

All AI initiatives undergo a pre-deployment impact assessment covering technical risk, business impact, regulatory compliance, and stakeholder notification.

Source: PR-91 v2.0

Transparency & Explainability

AI used in security, compliance, or decision-critical contexts must maintain audit trails, decision logs, and human-interpretable confidence scores.

Source: PR-91 v2.0

Human Oversight

High-risk AI decisions require human review checkpoints before any action is executed.

Source: PR-91 v2.0

Data Governance

Training data for internal AI models must be classified, minimised, and anonymised in line with ISO 27001.

Source: PR-91 v2.0
